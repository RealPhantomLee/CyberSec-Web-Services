Privacy Policy

Plain language. No buzzwords. Just the facts.

Effective date: May 2026 — Phantom Cyber Solutions

This policy describes what information Phantom Cyber Solutions collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. We keep this short because we actually read privacy policies and know you don't.

What We Collect

When you submit the contact form, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • The service you're interested in and your estimated budget
  • The message you write

We do not collect this information unless you actively submit it. Visiting the site does not require you to provide any personal data.

Browser Storage

This site uses localStorage (not cookies) for two purposes:

  • Cart: services you've added are stored locally in your browser so your selection persists between pages. This data never leaves your device unless you complete checkout.
  • Session analytics: basic page visit counts are stored locally to help us understand which pages are most useful. This data is not transmitted to any server.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.

How We Use Your Information

Contact form submissions are used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not:

  • Sell your information to third parties
  • Add you to a mailing list without your explicit consent
  • Use your data for advertising or profiling

Data Retention

Contact messages are retained for up to 90 days after initial contact, then deleted. If we enter into a working relationship, relevant communications may be kept for the duration of the engagement plus 12 months.

Third-Party Services

This site uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Cloudflare — DNS, CDN, and DDoS protection. Cloudflare processes request metadata (IP address, headers) to deliver the site. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — Fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which may log your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe — If you complete a purchase, payment is processed by Stripe. Phantom Cyber Solutions does not store your card details. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Security

All traffic is encrypted via HTTPS. Contact form submissions are transmitted securely to our server, which is hosted on-premises and not shared with third parties. We take security seriously — it's the whole point.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, use the contact form and specify your request. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the contact form. We'll give you a straight answer.
